# Open-science and blind-evaluation policy

ARANDU combines open artifacts with selective non-disclosure required for valid future blind experiments.

## Public after scientific exposure, where appropriate

The project aims to release:

- source code;
- public/consumed corpora;
- protocols and preregistration notes;
- frozen manifests and SHA-256 hashes;
- raw or terminal evidence;
- negative and technically inconclusive results;
- manuscript and supplementary sources.

## Temporarily non-public by design

The following may remain private until the relevant experiment is complete or public release cannot compromise future inference:

- private gold;
- unexposed confirmatory corpora;
- future units reserved for causal/path-dependence tests;
- credentials and operational secrets.

This boundary is not used to hide unfavorable historical results.

## Reviewer interpretation

Reviewers should treat absent future blind materials as embargoed experimental assets, while expecting every claim actually made in the manuscript to be traceable to released evidence or frozen cryptographic records.
